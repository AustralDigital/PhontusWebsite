# Phontus replacement image prompt set

All assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool. The hardware reference images in this folder controlled product geometry, and `live-interpretation-screen.png` was used as the exact display insert wherever two people were speaking through a kit.

## Shared hardware constraints

Frontline Kit: preserve the compact low white rounded base, one integrated tall tapered and gently curved white display housing, thick black landscape bezel, and the small trapezoidal silver speaker grille below the screen. Do not add a detached tablet, tablet arm, handset wells, storage, green trim, extra controls, or invented branding.

Clinical Kit: preserve the white five-caster star base, one brushed-silver post, one compact square white cabinet with rounded corners, a black battery panel at upper left, tissue-box niche at upper right, wide open lower cubby, and the tapered screen housing with its silver trapezoidal speaker grille. Do not add trays, stacked cabinets, glass doors, loose tablets, cables, green trim, or invented branding.

All scenes: premium photorealistic editorial photography, natural expressions and skin, restrained warm neutrals and deep-green accents, no headsets, no watermark, no readable environmental signage. Perspective-match the supplied Phontus Live Interpretation screen inside the device bezel, retaining the white interface, green/yellow waveform, and red End Session button.
